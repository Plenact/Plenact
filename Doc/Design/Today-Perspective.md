# A little perspective

The lower Today section offers optional perspective after capture, Focus, and the
user’s pinned shortcuts. It is not another ranked task feed or a required step.

- **Close the loop** hosts the existing daily and weekly review invitations and
  Planning rhythm controls. Timing, Later, Skip, opt-in notifications, and manual
  review routes remain governed by Review Rhythm settings.
- **Worth noticing** appears only when today’s canonical Day list contains
  completed non-divider items. It shows a count and the first two in source order,
  then opens Today’s Plan for reflection. These are completed items on that list,
  not claims that completion happened today. No streak, score, or inferred emotion.
- **A glance at tomorrow** reads the dated calendar’s next local day, including
  week boundaries. It shows up to two unfinished non-divider items in source order
  and the remaining count. Absent and empty lists have different copy. Its action
  opens Tomorrow’s Plan; browsing the panel does not create or move content.

Panels use Today’s existing translucent surfaces, short sentences, readable labels,
and one main next step. The completion panel disappears when irrelevant. Essential
reminders retain their existing paths; no action depends on discovering this area.

This first pass does not invent summaries, infer recent wins from completion flags,
rank overdue work, or automatically carry anything into another day.

## Moving between the two spaces

The outer Today scroll view uses native scroll target settling. A deliberate upward
swipe crossing the perspective reveal boundary settles at the start of perspective,
clamped to the available scroll range. A downward swipe crossing back above the
shortcut captions settles at the top. Short movements and scrolling within either
space keep their ordinary destinations. Focus’s nested list has no snap behavior.
Reduce Motion disables this optional snap and retains ordinary scrolling.
