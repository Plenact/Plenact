# Plenact

**Taking your plan into action.**

Plenact is building an approachable planning and organization experience for daily tasks, routines, ideas, personal projects, and open work. The product begins with a calm **Today** front door and a flexible **Board** inspired by practical kanban planning.

## Why Plenact

Planning tools can become another source of work. Plenact is exploring a clearer path from deciding what matters to taking the next useful action:

- **Orient quickly:** begin with today's plan instead of scanning an entire system.
- **Plan visually:** organize work in ordered lists and cards while preserving personal structure.
- **Reduce cognitive load:** favor readable information, plain language, predictable navigation, and visible actions.
- **Adapt respectfully:** support different planning styles without making medical or treatment claims.

Plenact is informed by lessons from successful traumatic brain injury recovery and is being designed for broad use by individuals and, over time, teams and recovery-oriented settings.

## Current Development

Plenact is in early iOS development. The current app includes:

- A Today view that opens directly into an existing day or work list.
- A horizontally navigable kanban Board with ordered lists, cards, and section dividers.
- Card details with completion, dates, checklists, comments, members, labels, and attachments.
- Local persistence on the current app installation.

Calendar synchronization, accounts, cloud/team synchronization, a remote database, and web or desktop clients are not currently implemented.

## Product Principles

- Calm, clear, and predictable by default.
- Fast access to common actions and the work that matters now.
- Dynamic Type, VoiceOver, contrast, and comfortable touch targets considered from the beginning.
- Personal and shared content treated as meaningfully different concepts.
- Honest product boundaries: proposed capabilities are not presented as available features.
